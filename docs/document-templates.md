# DAVEBOT Document Templates

This document outlines the content blocks needed for branded quotes and job packs. The system should generate PDFs with company branding.

## Quote Document

### Header

- Company logo
- Company name, address, phone, email
- Quote number, date, validity
- Customer name and address

### Scope Summary

- Job type and tool description
- Cavity count and key assumptions

### Cost Breakdown

- Labor
- Material
- Standard components
- Overhead
- Margin

### Terms & Conditions

- Lead time estimate
- Payment terms
- Revision policy
- Exclusions and assumptions

### Signature / Acceptance

- Authorized signature line
- PO reference field

## Job Pack (Internal)

- Job number and customer details
- Routing steps and planned hours
- BOM and inventory reservations
- Drawings and revision history
- Inspection and sampling checklist
